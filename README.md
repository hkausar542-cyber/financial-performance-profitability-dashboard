# Financial Performance & Profitability Dashboard (Power BI)

A Power BI dashboard that analyzes revenue, net profit and net margin across product categories, regions and years.

## Dashboard Preview
![Financial Performance Dashboard](Screenshot%202026-11-16%20114149.png)

## Objective
Track revenue and profitability over time and find which product categories earn the best margins.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX measures
- Data cleaning and modelling

## Key Metrics
- Total Revenue: 164.15M
- Total Net Profit: 23.52M
- Net Margin: 14.33%

## Key Insights
- Apparel has the highest average net margin (about 20%), followed by Furniture and Electronics
- Home Appliances has the lowest average net margin (about 10%)
- Revenue and net profit both grow every year from 2023 to 2025
- Year and Region slicers let users filter the whole dashboard
